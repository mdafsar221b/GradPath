# GradPath Submission Flow

## Final Title

GradPath: A Syllabus-Mapped Previous Year Question Analysis and Model Paper Generation System for BCA Students

## One-Line Problem Statement

BCA notes and previous-year papers are usually scattered across PDFs, drives, and chats, so students and faculty do not have a structured system to organize them by syllabus, analyze exam patterns, or generate subject-specific model papers.

## Core Contribution

The project does not depend on generic AI alone. Its main contribution is converting unorganized academic material into structured academic data that can be searched, curated, analyzed, and reused for exam preparation.

## Main Flow

1. Admin uploads notes and PYQ resources into the system.
2. The system stores them against semester, subject, and unit metadata.
3. PYQ papers are curated into structured question entries.
4. Students open the same curated data through the library and practice workspace.
5. The system analyzes repeated topics, marks distribution, and historical question patterns.
6. A model paper is generated from curated local PYQ history.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the admin resource workflow and show PYQ/resource upload or existing curated entries.
2. Show that resources are tied to semester, subject, and unit.
3. Open a curated PYQ paper and show the structured question data.
4. Open the practice workspace and show topic-wise or PYQ-wise practice.
5. Open the PYQ Analysis page and show important topics plus pattern signals.
6. Generate the model paper and explain that it comes from curated historical data, not generic prompting alone.

## What To Say If Asked "What Problem Does It Solve?"

It solves the problem of scattered academic material and unstructured previous-year papers by turning them into a syllabus-aware system that supports resource discovery, PYQ analysis, and model paper generation for BCA exam preparation.

## What To Say If Asked "Why Not Just Use ChatGPT?"

ChatGPT can answer generic questions, but it does not maintain a department-specific, syllabus-mapped repository of notes and PYQs, does not curate and organize local academic material automatically, and does not analyze historical paper patterns from the institution's own data unless that data is structured first.

## What To Emphasize

- syllabus-linked resource organization
- PYQ curation
- topic and marks pattern analysis
- model paper generation from curated local data

## What To Mention As Supporting Features Only

- AI tutor
- quizzes
- flashcards
- assignments
